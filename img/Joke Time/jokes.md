# 😂 Jokes Collection

A collection of random, relatable, and painfully accurate jokes about student life, everyday struggles, and Filipino humor.

---

## English Jokes

### 1. Brain.exe Has Stopped Working

**Q:** Why am I always tired in class?

**A:** Because my brain is still loading... ⏳

### 2. Financial Crisis

**Q:** Why is my wallet always empty?

**A:** Because every time I open it, there's nothing inside. 💸

### 3. The Night Owl

**Me:** I'll sleep early tonight.

**Also me at 2 AM:** "One more video!" 📱

### 4. Online 24/7

**Friend:** Why are you always online?

**Me:** Because my responsibilities are offline. 💀

### 5. Future Plans

**Friend:** What's your plan for the future?

**Me:** Survive today first. We'll talk about tomorrow later. 😭

### 6. The Power of Smiling

**Friend:** Why are you always smiling?

**Me:** Because crying doesn't solve my problems, and neither does smiling, so why not? 😂

## Filipino Jokes

### 7. StiffNeck Curry

**Tanong:** Sinong sikat na NBA player ang laging sumasakit ang leeg pagkatapos maglaro?

**Sagot:** Eh di si... **StiffNeck Curry!** 🏀

### 8. Chess Checkmate

**Tanong:** Bakit palaging sawi sa pag-ibig ang mga seryosong Chess player?

**Sagot:** Kasi bago pa man sila manligaw, iniisip na agad nila kung paano sila ma-**Checkmate!** ♟️

### 9. BAT-MINTON

**Tanong:** Ano ang paboritong sport ni Dracula at ng mga bampira?

**Sagot:** Eh di... **BAT-MINTON!** 🦇

### 10. Boxing Ring

**Tanong:** Bakit parisukat ang hugis ng Boxing Ring kahit "ring" ang tawag dito?

**Sagot:** Kasi kapag ginawa nilang bilog, baka mag-roll out ang mga boksingero! 🥊

### 11. Golf and the Rich

**Tanong:** Bakit paboritong sport ng mga mayayaman ang Golf?

**Sagot:** Kasi kahit magkamali sila ng palo, okay lang... sanay naman silang magbayad ng "Penalty Shot" sa buhay! ⛳
