My Favorite Movie

Five films I can watch on repeat. Pick a poster to see the story, cast and characters.

Free Guy (2021)

Action · Comedy · Sci-Fi — directed by Shawn Levy

Synopsis

Guy is a cheerful bank teller who slowly realizes he is actually a background character in an open-world video game called Free City. When he starts breaking his own script to help a player named Molotov Girl, he becomes the hero of the game — and the key to saving it.

Cast

  • Ryan Reynolds — Guy
  • Jodie Comer — Millie / Molotov Girl
  • Joe Keery — Keys
  • Lil Rel Howery — Buddy
  • Utkarsh Ambudkar — Mouser
  • Taika Waititi — Antwan

Characters

  • Guy — a kind NPC who becomes self-aware
  • Millie — a game developer playing as Molotov Girl
  • Keys — Millie's friend and coder
  • Buddy — Guy's loyal best friend
  • Antwan — the egotistical game company boss

Clip slot: put a clip named free_guy.mp4 in the videos folder.

Jumanji: Welcome to the Jungle (2017)

Adventure · Comedy — directed by Jake Kasdan

Synopsis

Four teenagers in detention find an old video game console and are sucked into the jungle world of Jumanji, where they become the adult avatars they chose. They have to work together, use their strengths, and survive three lives each to beat the game and go home.

Cast

  • Dwayne Johnson — Dr. Smolder Bravestone
  • Kevin Hart — Franklin "Mouse" Finbar
  • Jack Black — Professor Shelly Oberon
  • Karen Gillan — Ruby Roundhouse
  • Nick Jonas — Jefferson "Seaplane" McDonough
  • Bobby Cannavale — Russell Van Pelt

Characters

  • Spencer / Bravestone — a shy gamer in a hero's body
  • Fridge / Mouse — the football star turned sidekick
  • Bethany / Oberon — the selfie queen as a middle-aged expert
  • Martha / Ruby — the shy girl as a fierce fighter
  • Van Pelt — the villain who controls the jungle's beasts

Clip slot: put a clip named jumanji.mp4 in the videos folder.

Shrek 2 (2004)

Animation · Comedy · Fantasy — directed by Andrew Adamson, Kelly Asbury & Conrad Vernon

Synopsis

Back from their honeymoon, Shrek and Fiona are invited to the kingdom of Far Far Away to meet her parents. The king is shocked that his daughter married an ogre, and a scheming Fairy Godmother wants Fiona for her own son. With Donkey and a new friend, Puss in Boots, Shrek fights to keep his family together.

Cast

  • Mike Myers — Shrek
  • Eddie Murphy — Donkey
  • Cameron Diaz — Fiona
  • Antonio Banderas — Puss in Boots
  • Julie Andrews — Queen Lillian
  • John Cleese — King Harold
  • Jennifer Saunders — Fairy Godmother
  • Rupert Everett — Prince Charming

Characters

  • Shrek — the grumpy but kind-hearted ogre
  • Fiona — the princess who chose to stay an ogre
  • Donkey — the talkative best friend
  • Puss in Boots — a swashbuckling cat hired to stop Shrek
  • Fairy Godmother — the manipulative villain

Clip slot: put a clip named shrek2.mp4 in the videos folder.

The Mitchells vs. the Machines (2021)

Animation · Comedy · Sci-Fi — directed by Mike Rianda

Synopsis

Katie Mitchell is a quirky film-maker about to leave for film school, so her dad plans one last cross-country road trip with the whole family. When a tech company's AI, PAL, starts a robot uprising and captures humanity, the weird Mitchells become the world's last hope.

Cast

  • Abbi Jacobson — Katie Mitchell
  • Danny McBride — Rick Mitchell
  • Maya Rudolph — Linda Mitchell
  • Mike Rianda — Aaron Mitchell
  • Eric André — Mark Bowman
  • Olivia Colman — PAL

Characters

  • Katie — the dreamy, creative older daughter
  • Rick — the over-protective, tech-fearing dad
  • Linda — the upbeat peacemaker mom
  • Aaron — the dinosaur-obsessed little brother
  • Monchi — the family's very strange pug
  • PAL — the AI assistant turned villain

Clip slot: put a clip named mitchells.mp4 in the videos folder.

Thor: Ragnarok (2017)

Action · Comedy · Fantasy — directed by Taika Waititi

Synopsis

Thor is stranded on the junkyard planet Sakaar without his hammer and forced to fight as a gladiator — against the Hulk. He has to escape and get back to Asgard in time to stop Hela, the goddess of death, from destroying his home and bringing about Ragnarok.

Cast

  • Chris Hemsworth — Thor
  • Tom Hiddleston — Loki
  • Cate Blanchett — Hela
  • Mark Ruffalo — Bruce Banner / Hulk
  • Tessa Thompson — Valkyrie
  • Jeff Goldblum — Grandmaster
  • Idris Elba — Heimdall
  • Anthony Hopkins — Odin
  • Taika Waititi — Korg

Characters

  • Thor — the God of Thunder, now without his hammer
  • Loki — his tricky adopted brother
  • Hela — the powerful villain and Thor's sister
  • Valkyrie — a hard-drinking former Asgardian warrior
  • Hulk — the champion of Sakaar
  • Korg — the polite rock-monster gladiator

Clip slot: put a clip named thor_ragnarok.mp4 in the videos folder.
