Bulalo

Beef shank & bone-marrow soup

Ingredients (4–6 servings)

  • 1.5 kg beef shank with bone marrow
  • 2 liters water
  • 1 large onion, quartered
  • 1 tbsp whole peppercorns
  • 2 corn cobs, cut into 3 pieces each
  • 1 small cabbage or 1 bunch pechay
  • A handful of green beans (optional)
  • 2–3 tbsp fish sauce, salt to taste
  • Dip: fish sauce + calamansi + chili

Cooking instructions

  1. Boil the beef in a pot of water for 10 minutes, then drain and rinse to remove the scum.
  2. Return the beef to the pot with fresh water, the onion and the peppercorns.
  3. Simmer on low for 2–2.5 hours until the meat is fork-tender (about 45 minutes in a pressure cooker).
  4. Add the corn and cook for 10 minutes.
  5. Season with fish sauce. Add the green beans, then the cabbage or pechay, and cook 3 more minutes.
  6. Serve hot with the marrow still in the bone and the dip on the side.

Crispy Pata

Deep-fried pork hock

Ingredients (4–6 servings)

  • 1 pork hock (pata), about 1.3 kg
  • 1 head garlic, halved
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp peppercorns
  • 2 tbsp salt, plus more for rubbing
  • About 1 liter cooking oil
  • Sauce: soy sauce, vinegar, minced garlic, chili

Cooking instructions

  1. Boil the pork hock with garlic, onion, bay leaves, peppercorns and salt for 1.5–2 hours until tender.
  2. Drain, pat dry, and let it sit uncovered in the fridge for at least 4 hours (overnight is best) so the skin dries out.
  3. Rub the skin with salt.
  4. Heat the oil in a deep pot to about 175 °C. Lower the pata in carefully and fry 15–20 minutes, turning, until golden and blistered.
  5. Drain on a rack, rest for 5 minutes, then chop and serve with the vinegar-soy dip.

Careful: Hot oil spits and pops. Use a splatter screen or lid and keep the skin very dry.

Kare-Kare

Oxtail in rich peanut sauce

Ingredients (4–6 servings)

  • 1 kg oxtail (or beef shank / tripe)
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup ground roasted peanuts
  • 1/4 cup toasted ground rice
  • 2 tbsp annatto (atsuete) seeds soaked in 1/2 cup warm water
  • 1 onion and 4 cloves garlic
  • 2 eggplants, 1 bunch string beans, 1 bunch pechay
  • Fish sauce or salt to taste
  • Bagoong (shrimp paste) for serving

Cooking instructions

  1. Boil the oxtail in water for 2.5–3 hours (about 1 hour in a pressure cooker) until very tender. Keep 3 cups of the broth.
  2. Sauté garlic and onion in a clean pot until soft.
  3. Squeeze the annatto in its water, strain, and pour the orange water into the pot.
  4. Stir in the peanut butter, ground peanuts and toasted rice, then add the oxtail and the 3 cups of broth.
  5. Simmer 20 minutes, stirring so it doesn't stick, until thick.
  6. Add the eggplant and string beans, cook 5 minutes, then the pechay for 2 minutes. Season to taste.
  7. Serve with warm rice and a spoon of bagoong on the side.

Lechon Kawali

Crispy pan-fried pork belly

Ingredients (4 servings)

  • 1 kg pork belly, in one slab
  • 1 onion, 1 head garlic, 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp peppercorns
  • 1 tbsp salt, plus more for rubbing
  • Water to cover
  • About 1 liter cooking oil
  • Dip: vinegar, soy sauce, chopped onion and chili

Cooking instructions

  1. Boil the pork belly with the onion, garlic, bay leaves, peppercorns and salt for 40–50 minutes until tender.
  2. Drain and pat completely dry. Air-dry or chill uncovered for 1–2 hours (overnight for extra crunch).
  3. Rub the skin with salt.
  4. Heat the oil to about 175 °C and fry skin-side down for 15–20 minutes, turning once, until deep golden and bubbly.
  5. Rest 5 minutes, chop into chunks and serve with the vinegar dip.

Careful: Keep a lid nearby — wet skin makes the oil spit.

Sisig

Sizzling chopped pork with calamansi

Ingredients (3–4 servings)

  • 500 g pork belly (or pork face / ears)
  • 1 large red onion, finely chopped
  • 3–5 siling labuyo, chopped
  • 3 tbsp calamansi juice
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp butter or oil
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise (optional)
  • 1 egg per serving, salt and pepper

Cooking instructions

  1. Boil the pork with salt and peppercorns for about 40 minutes until tender.
  2. Grill or broil the pork until the outside is charred and crisp.
  3. Chop it into very small pieces.
  4. Melt the butter in a very hot pan, cook half the onion until fragrant, then add the pork and fry until crisp at the edges.
  5. Add soy sauce, calamansi, chili and the rest of the onion. Stir in the mayonnaise if using, and season with salt and pepper.
  6. Serve on a sizzling plate and crack a raw egg on top. Mix it in at the table.
